\section{Results III: Training Convergence}
\label{sec:results-convergence}

Figure~\ref{fig:loss-curves} plots training loss (log scale) against Adam/L-BFGS iteration for the
classical baseline and the baseline hybrid configuration ($n_{\text{qubits}}=4$, $n_{\text{qlayers}}=2$,
Section~\ref{sec:setup}), for all three boundary conditions and both the forward and inverse problem
(Section~\ref{sec:background}). This is a sanity check that the hybrid model reaches a comparable loss at
all, independent of the per-step cost differences reported in Section~\ref{sec:results-timing}.
