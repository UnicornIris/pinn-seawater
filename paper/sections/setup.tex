\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Timed stages}
We time three cumulative stages on the same batch of collocation points:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{forward}: evaluate $\Th(x)$;
  \item \textbf{first\_grad}: the forward pass plus $\nabla_x\Th$;
  \item \textbf{second\_grad}: the forward pass, $\nabla_x\Th$ and $\partial^2\Th/\partial z^2$.
\end{enumerate}
The stages are cumulative, so the \emph{second-over-forward ratio} is the cost of everything needed for the
PDE residual relative to a plain evaluation. The parameter gradient $\partial\Lloss/\partial\theta$ is not part
of these stages, so a full training step costs more than the second\_grad time.

\subsection{Protocol}
Each stage is timed over \todo{20} repetitions after \todo{3} warm-up calls, on a batch of \todo{2540}
collocation points sampled uniformly in $[0,2]\times[0,5]$, matching the training batch size.
Network weights, batch data and circuit parameters are redrawn for each of 3 independent seeds; we report the
mean and the standard deviation across seeds. Backends are \texttt{default.qubit} (vectorised over the batch)
